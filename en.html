Viborg Board Game Day 2026

A relaxed day where you can learn a new game, borrow from the board game library, join competitions, try role-playing, miniature painting and much more.

The day is aimed at adults and young people. If you are under 15, Klubberne i Viborg (the local youth clubs) run role-playing, miniature painting and other game activities for children and young people at the same time and place. Read more

Ticket & payment

One ticket. The whole day.

Entry is DKK 50 for the whole day. It gives access to the open gaming tables, the board game library, activities, competitions, prize draws and lunch. Some activities require a separate (free) booking of a seat.

After you sign up, you pay via MobilePay Box. We don't want 50 kroner to keep anyone away, so you can also tick the box to take part for free.

Don't have MobilePay, or stuck on the form? Write to us at viborgbraetspil@gmail.com and we'll help.

Booking activities: once you're signed up you can book the activities with limited seats. Booking is also in Danish ("Book aktiviteter").

First time?

Just come on in.

Technically, Viborg Board Game Day 2026 is a first for everyone, because it's the first time we hold it. Come in a good mood, and we'll help each other have a cozy, relaxed and down-to-earth day together. Sitting down at a table with new people is also a great way to get to know others in Viborg.

No plan needed

You don't need to play lots of board games every day, know the rules, know other participants or plan the day in advance. You can also just show up and see what you feel like.

Come alone or with others

You don't need a fixed gaming group or to know anyone. If you've booked an activity, just turn up for it. Otherwise our volunteers will help you find a game or an activity.

Learn the rules on the day

Many activities are learn-to-play sessions where the host explains the game and helps you get started. We don't know the rules of everything either, but we'll help as best we can.

When you arrive

Start at check-in by the entrance to Ungdomsskolen, Reberbanen 11 + 13, 8800 Viborg. Then go to an activity you've booked, visit the library, find an open table or ask a volunteer.

No plan? That's a plan too

A couple of hundred games are waiting in the board game library. Borrow a game, find a table and play.

Stay as long as you like

Come in the morning, come later, leave and come back. The day mixes planned activities and open play.

Language

The day is in Danish

Activities, game explanations and announcements are all in Danish, and we don't have the resources to offer help in English on the day. If you're comfortable with that, you're very welcome. Many games are easy to follow by watching, and playing with others is a relaxed way to meet new people and grow your local network.

Next year

Help us make it more international

We'd love to offer more in English in the future, but it takes people to make it happen. Would you like to host an activity in English, teach a game or help in other ways next time? Write to us at viborgbraetspil@gmail.com.

Children and young people

Klubberne i Viborg

Our activities are mainly aimed at adults, so we're very happy that Klubberne i Viborg (the local youth clubs) are coming with their own activities for children and young people in grades 4 to 7 (grades 8 to 9 are also welcome): role-playing, miniature painting, games, drop-in activities and some activities at set times with limited seats.

Children under 15 must be accompanied by an adult who is responsible for them throughout the event, including during the clubs' activities. We don't offer separate supervision of children.

Prizes

Win board games

Some games are drawn among participants just for showing up, and you can win games in several of the day's competitions. Everyone signed up is automatically in the draws, one entry per person.

Prizes are handed out in person on the day at the joint draw at 10:05. You must be present to receive your prize; prizes are not posted or handed to others. If a winner isn't there, the game goes into a new draw among those present.

Volunteer

Want to lend a hand?

Viborg Board Game Day is made by volunteers. Help for an hour, a few hours or the whole day: teach a game you know, help at the library or the kiosk, welcome participants, or help with setting up and clearing away.

Volunteers get free entry, a volunteer T-shirt, food and drinks, and time to play too. Write to viborgbraetspil@gmail.com and tell us a little about yourself.

Safety

A nice and safe place to be

We expect everyone to treat each other with respect. Harassment, threatening behaviour, discrimination or other behaviour that makes others feel unsafe is not accepted. You can always stop playing a game or leave an activity.

If you experience something unpleasant or feel unsafe, talk to the organisers or a volunteer. We'll help handle the situation and make sure you have a good day.

Questions & answers

Frequently asked questions

Here are the answers to the most common questions about the day.

Is the day in English?

No. Viborg Board Game Day is run in Danish. Activities, rules explanations and announcements are in Danish, and we can't offer help in English on the day. You're very welcome to join, but be prepared that everything happens in Danish. If you'd like to help make the day more international next year, please get in touch.

Can I come alone or with a group?

Yes. You're very welcome to come alone or with a group. We'll help you find other participants, an open table or an activity you can join.

Do I have to be good at board games?

No. One of the organisers, André, is in fact terrible at board games, but that doesn't stop him. You don't need experience or to know the games beforehand. Many activities are designed so you can learn the game on the spot, and at the open tables our volunteers are happy to get you started.

Can you teach me the rules?

Yes. At the learn-to-play activities a host explains the game and plays along, and at the open tables you can always ask a volunteer.

Do I have to stay all day?

Come for a couple of hours or stay all day. You can come and go as you like... but we'd really love for you to stay a bit longer (Fact™).

Is the day only for experienced players?

The day is for beginners, casual players and board game enthusiasts alike. Just sign up for the activity that best fits your level of ambition.

What's included in the ticket?

Access to the whole event, lunch, the board game library, open gaming tables, activities, a cozy atmosphere and new acquaintances... and maybe a prize?! :-o

Can I buy food and drinks?

Lunch is rye bread, cold cuts and vegetables, and it's included in the price. Dinner is filled pita bread and costs DKK 25. You order dinner when you sign up and get a meal ticket at check-in. Coffee, soft drinks, snacks, cake and more are also for sale on the day. You can pay with MobilePay or exact change in cash.

Can I bring my own food and drinks?

Yes. We'll also try to sell snacks, soft drinks and coffee at friendly prices (friendly for you, that is, since we're not out to make money on the event). Pay with MobilePay or exact change in cash, as we can't guarantee change.

Can I eat snacks at the gaming tables?

No, unfortunately not. The reason is simple: the games are kindly lent to us by private individuals, so please take good care of them! Food and drinks can be enjoyed in unlimited quantities in other areas.

Can I borrow board games on the day?

Yes. We have an open board game library with a couple of hundred games you can borrow and take to a free table. When you're done, just return the game so others can enjoy it. Please take good care of the games, as they are lent by private individuals!

What if I don't know what to play?

Then ask us. Tell us how many you are, how much time you have, and whether you want something light, strategic, co-operative or competitive. We'll gladly help you find something from the library or point you to an activity.

Can I bring my own board games?

Yes, please do. You're welcome to bring your own games and play them at the open tables. Games you bring are your own responsibility.

Can I bring a friend?

Yes. You can come alone, as a couple, with a friend or with a whole gaming group. Everyone just needs to be signed up for Viborg Board Game Day and have paid entry.

How do I book an activity?

First, sign up for Viborg Board Game Day. Signed-up participants can then book activities with limited seats, so sign up in good time. You can ONLY book activities once you're signed up for the day. Drop-in activities need no separate booking; just show up once you're signed up.

Can I play without booking an activity?

Yes, definitely. You can use the board game library, the open tables and the drop-in activities without booking anything. Booking is only needed for activities with a limited number of seats.

What do I do when I arrive?

Start at check-in by the entrance to Ungdomsskolen at Reberbanen 11 + 13 and let us know you've arrived. Then head to an activity you've booked, visit the library, find an open table or ask a volunteer for help.

Can I leave and come back later?

Yes. Once you've checked in, you're welcome to leave and come back later the same day.

Is there an age limit?

Everyone is welcome, and children are very welcome together with adults. Our activities are mainly aimed at young people and adults, while Klubberne i Viborg have a separate programme for children and young people. Everyone can borrow games, such as family games, from the library and find a spot at the open tables. Children under 15 must be accompanied by an adult who is responsible for them, as we don't offer separate supervision.

Is the event accessible?

Yes. The rooms can be used by wheelchair users and people with reduced mobility. If you have special needs or need help with your visit, you're very welcome to contact us in advance at viborgbraetspil@gmail.com.

Can I bring pets?

We love animals, but no. No pets at the event.

Who is behind Viborg Board Game Day?

Viborg Board Game Day is a local, volunteer-run initiative created by private individuals with an interest in board games, role-playing and community. On the day, volunteer hosts help with activities, learn-to-play sessions, tournaments, the library, practical tasks and helping people find someone to play with. It is not a commercial fair. Entry fees go to the event, the volunteers and developing Viborg Board Game Day. Anders and André Højer are the main organisers; contact us at viborgbraetspil@gmail.com.

How do you use my personal data?

When you sign up, we collect the information needed to manage your registration, any activity bookings and practical communication about the event. It is only used for Viborg Board Game Day and is not shared with sponsors or other external parties for marketing. Only people who need it to run the event have access. It is deleted when it's no longer needed for running and following up on the event (including a reminder about next year's event). To see your data or have it deleted, contact viborgbraetspil@gmail.com. Full privacy policy (Danish): Privatliv.

Will photos be taken on the day?

We can't rule out that people take photos during the day, and we'd like a few for the website, the Facebook page and future information about the event. We'll ask before taking photos where individuals are clearly the subject. You can always say no to being photographed or ask us not to use a photo of you. If you find a photo of yourself afterwards that you'd like removed, contact viborgbraetspil@gmail.com and we'll remove it right away!

I have more questions

Please get in touch at viborgbraetspil@gmail.com with any questions, ideas, feedback or anything else!
