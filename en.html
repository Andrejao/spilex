Thank you for Viborg Brætspilsdag 2026

We held Viborg Board Game Day for the first time, and around 300 people took part over the course of the day. Thank you to everyone who came and played, to the volunteers, to everyone who ran an activity, and to everyone who supported the day.

We will be sharing photos from the day over the coming weeks.
